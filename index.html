Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Pulse is a social photo-sharing app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. What we collect

When you use Pulse we store: your username, display name, bio and profile photo; the photos, captions, comments, likes and stories you post; the songs you add to the shared library; the messages you send to other users; and your follows and saved posts. If you sign up with email or Google, we also store your email address.

2. Where it is stored

Your content is stored in a Supabase cloud database and its file storage. Photos and songs you upload are viewable by anyone who uses Pulse — that is the point of a social app. Do not upload anything you want to keep private.

3. On your device

We save your login session and small preferences (like which stories you have seen) in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

4. Google sign-in

If you tap “Continue with Google”, Google shares your basic account details (name and email) with us so we can sign you in. We never see or store your Google password.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not build advertising profiles.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your posts, stories and messages at any time inside the app. To delete your account and everything in it, email luckychouhan4404@gmail.com and your profile, posts and messages will be removed.

7. Children

Pulse is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Continuing to use Pulse after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions? Email luckychouhan4404@gmail.com.
